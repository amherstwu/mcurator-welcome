MCurator Privacy Policy / 隱私權政策

Last updated: May 28, 2026 · 最後更新：2026 年 5 月 28 日 · Bundle ID: com.mcurator.mobile

Demo platform / 示範平台

MCurator is a demonstration platform for specimen curation and the SAT marketplace. Mock identities simulate custody transfer only and are not legal, financial, or museum accession credentials.

MCurator 為科學標本管理與 SAT 市集示範平台；「模擬身分」僅供保管權轉移演示，不構成真實金融、法律或博物館典藏憑證。

English

Who we are

MCurator Mobile (Bundle ID: com.mcurator.mobile) helps you create field records, digital specimen passports, and browse the SAT marketplace demo.

Data we process

Depending on features you use, the app may process: specimen photos from the camera, images you pick from the photo library, GPS coordinates while in use (field records), and specimen or marketplace demo data you enter. Language preference is stored locally on your device (localStorage).

Why we request permissions

Camera: capture field specimen records. Photo library: import existing specimen images. Location (while in use): geotag collection records. We do not track your location in the background.

Storage and sharing

Demo data is primarily kept on your device or in your app session. Taxonomy or locality enrichment may query public APIs such as Open-Meteo or Nominatim (no personal identifiers). We do not sell your personal data.

Your choices

You may deny camera, photo library, or location permissions in system settings; related features will be unavailable. Uninstalling the app clears locally stored data.

Children’s privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be announced in the app or on our support page. Continued use means you accept the updated version.

Contact: support@mcurator.app

繁體中文

我們是誰

MCurator Mobile（Bundle ID：com.mcurator.mobile）由 MCurator 團隊提供，協助使用者建立標本野外紀錄、數位護照與瀏覽 SAT 市集示範內容。

我們收集的資料

依您使用的功能，App 可能處理：相機拍攝的標本照片、相簿中您選擇的圖片、使用中的 GPS 座標（野外紀錄）、以及您在 App 內輸入的標本與市集示範資料。語言偏好儲存在裝置本地（localStorage）。

權限用途

相機：拍攝野外標本紀錄。相簿：匯入既有標本照片。定位（使用 App 期間）：為採集紀錄加上地理標記。我們不會在背景持續追蹤您的位置。

資料儲存與分享

示範版資料主要保存在您的裝置或 App 工作階段中。若您使用分類證據或地點 enrichment 功能，App 可能向 Open-Meteo、Nominatim 等公開 API 查詢氣候或地名（不含個人識別資訊）。我們不出售您的個人資料。

您的選擇

您可在系統設定中拒絕相機、相簿或定位權限；相關功能將無法使用。您可刪除 App 以清除本地儲存資料。

兒童隱私

本 App 非針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，我們不 knowingly 收集兒童個人資料。

政策變更

我們可能更新本政策；重大變更將於 App 或支援頁面公告。繼續使用即表示您接受更新後的版本。

聯絡：support@mcurator.app
